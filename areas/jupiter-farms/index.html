First Problem

When the Well Pump or Septic Tank Is the Emergency

In most of Jupiter Farms there is no town water line, and most lots have no sewer main either, though the Loxahatchee River District does list parts of Jupiter Farms in its sewer service area. A typical house runs on a private well and a septic tank, so the failure points are different.

No water usually means a pump, a pressure switch, or a pressure tank. A slow drain throughout the house can mean a full tank or a saturated drain field rather than a clog.

The first shutoff is not at a meter. It is the breaker for the well pump and the valves around the pressure tank. If a pipe is leaking, switch off the pump breaker so the system does not keep pushing water into it. Then call the 24/7 line.

Emergency Plumber Jupiter Farms FL 33478: Well, Septic, or House Line?

On acreage, the most useful thing a caller can say is where the water comes from. Tell dispatch if the home runs on a well and pressure tank, and where the septic tank sits relative to the house.

Lots here run 1.25 to 2.5 acres, so driveways are long. Give the street name and any gate details before the crew heads out, because a technician who misses the turn at 2 a.m. loses time you do not have.

If the pump runs but nothing comes out of the faucets, the cause could be the well, the pressure tank, or a burst line between them. The 24/7 line sorts that out on the phone before anyone drives to 33478.

The Lots

1.25-Acre Lots, Agricultural Zoning, and Long Pipe Runs

Jupiter Farms was platted mostly in the 1950s and 1960s into lots of roughly 1.25 to 2.5 acres, and most of it is zoned Agricultural Residential. About 12,500 people lived there at the 2020 census. It is unincorporated Palm Beach County, so permits come from the county and septic oversight from the Florida Department of Health in Palm Beach County. Plumbing work follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Large lots mean long pipe runs. A supply line from a pump house to a barn, a pool, or an outbuilding can run a long way underground. A leak there may not show as a stain. It shows as a pump that runs too much.

Before We Arrive: If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak

Close the tank valve

Mind the yard
