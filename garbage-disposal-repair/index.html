Overview

Garbage Disposal Repair in Jupiter

A garbage disposal grinds food waste so it can pass through the drain. Repair covers jams, a motor that hums but does not spin, leaks at the flange or housing, and clogs in the line after it. Installation covers swapping a failed unit for a new one and connecting it to the sink drain, power, and the dishwasher.

Never put a hand into a disposal. Most jams clear with a hex wrench at the bottom of the unit, and a reset button on the underside can clear a tripped motor.

Jupiter Conditions

Septic Tanks, Sewer, and Disposals

In Jupiter proper, the Loxahatchee River District treats wastewater from the sewer system, and disposals feed that system without issue if used sensibly. Grease is the main problem, because it hardens in the lateral and builds up over time.

On a septic tank, as in much of Jupiter Farms, a disposal adds solids that fill the tank faster. The Florida Department of Health recommends pumping every three to five years, and a household that uses a disposal may need pumping on the shorter end.

A dishwasher drain connects to the disposal or the sink tailpiece. The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), calls for a high loop or an air gap so dirty sink water cannot flow back into the dishwasher, and a missing loop is a common cause of odors and gurgling.

Decision

Repair or Replace the Disposal

Repair when

  • A jam is cleared and it runs
  • The leak is at the flange
  • The unit is only a few years old
  • The motor sounds strong

Replace when

  • The housing is leaking from the bottom
  • The motor is dead or burnt
  • The unit is more than about 8 to 12 years old
  • Jams keep coming back
Process

How an Installation Goes

1

Power off

The breaker is switched off and the old unit is disconnected.

2

Remove

The old unit and mounting assembly are taken out.

3

Mount

The new flange is sealed and the unit is mounted below the sink.

4

Connect

The drain, dishwasher hose with a high loop, and power are connected.

5

Test

Water and power are run to check flow, noise, and leaks.
