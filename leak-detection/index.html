Water Bill Up and No Visible Leak?

Many callers never use the words leak detection. They say the meter keeps moving, the bill doubled, or a ceiling stain appeared overnight. Each of those is a leak detection problem, and the first job is learning whether the water is escaping inside the house, in the yard, or on the street side of the meter.

That split decides who fixes it. Water lost between the meter and your house is usually the homeowner's repair. Water lost in the street main belongs to the utility.

Emergency

Emergency Leak Location: When Waiting Costs Money

A meter that runs with every tap closed, water appearing in a ceiling or floor, or a loss you can hear but cannot see are all reasons to call now. The leak does not pause overnight.

Close the house main to slow the loss and confirm with the meter. Then the crew uses acoustic, thermal, and pressure tools to pinpoint the spot, so the repair opens as little of the wall or floor as possible.

Overview

Leak Detection in Jupiter, Explained

Leak detection is the work of finding a hidden water leak without opening walls, floors, or yards at random. A technician uses a meter test, a pressure test, and listening or imaging equipment to narrow a leak to a small area first. Only then does anyone cut, dig, or repair.

In Jupiter the need shows up in predictable ways. A water bill that climbs with no change in habits, a damp patch on drywall, or a well pump that cycles when nobody is home all point the same direction. The line is answered 24/7, so you can describe what you see and get next steps at any hour.

Warning Signs

Signs of a Hidden Leak

A bill that jumps

Usage that rises with no change in habits is the most common first clue.

A meter that moves

A dial or low-flow indicator that keeps turning with every fixture closed means water is leaving somewhere.

Warm tile

A warm spot on a floor over a hot-water line suggests a leak in the slab.

Stains or bubbling paint

Brown rings, peeling paint, or soft drywall often trace back to a supply line behind the wall.

A musty smell

Constant dampness in one room can come from a leak and not just from Florida humidity.

Running water sounds

Hissing or trickling in a quiet house points to pressurized water escaping.

Process

How a Leak Gets Located

1

Meter test

Every fixture is closed and the meter is watched for movement. This separates a leak from normal use.

2

Isolate

Valves are closed section by section to learn whether the leak is on the hot side, the cold side, or outside.

3

Pressure test

A gauge on an isolated line shows whether pressure drops. A steady drop confirms a leak in that run.

4

Pinpoint

Listening gear, thermal imaging, or a moisture meter narrows the spot to a small area.

5

Report

You hear the location, the repair options, and the price before anything is cut.

Jupiter Conditions

Why Leaks Hide in Jupiter Homes

Jupiter grew fast. U.S. Census counts show the town rising from 3,136 residents in 1970 to 9,868 in 1980 and 24,986 in 1990. A large share of the housing stock dates to that stretch. Polybutylene supply pipe was widely installed in the United States from about 1978 to 1995, and homes from that window can carry it with fittings that fail without warning.

Salt air and humidity add wear to exposed valves, hose bibs, and fittings. That matters most near the coast and the Intracoastal Waterway, in parts of 33477 and 33469.

Slab-on-grade construction is the norm across South Florida, so supply lines often run through or under concrete. When one leaks, the water has no obvious place to show itself. That is why the meter test comes first. Pressure testing borrows from new construction, where the International Plumbing Code (IPC) requires water piping to be tested before it is covered. For what happens once a leak is traced under concrete, see the slab leak repair page.

Prep

Do This Before You Call

Find the meter

Lift the lid on the meter box near the street. The dial or low-flow indicator is what you watch.

Close every tap

Turn off appliances that use water, including the ice maker and any irrigation timer.

Read it twice

Write down the reading, wait at least 30 minutes with no water use, and read it again. Any change is useful on the phone.

Photograph stains

Take dated photos of any stain, damp spot, or warm floor for your records and any insurance claim.

Water Meter

Meter Still Running With Everything Off?

This is the single most common reason people phone for leak detection. Close every tap, the ice maker, and irrigation, then watch the meter's small leak indicator. If it moves, water is escaping somewhere. Closing the house main and checking again separates a leak inside from one on the line to the street.

If the bill rose with no change in habits, the high water bill guide walks through the meter test and the usual causes.

Walls and Ceilings

Ceiling Stains, Wall Leaks, and Water With No Visible Source

A stain on a ceiling often comes from the bathroom above or from an air conditioning drain line, and the pattern helps tell which. A leak inside a wall can show only as a damp baseboard or a smell. Acoustic and thermal tools locate the spot before drywall is opened.

The ceiling stain guide explains how to tell a plumbing leak from a drain line.

Yard and Irrigation

Irrigation Lines, Hot Tubs, and Buried Pipe

Sprinkler zones that run fast, a soggy patch above a line, or a spot near a hot tub can hide a buried leak. Isolating each zone and pressure testing the line is faster than digging. Irrigation backflow assemblies are handled under backflow testing.

Mobile and manufactured homes can have supply lines run beneath the floor, and they get the same pressure test and locate approach.

Related Work

When the Leak Turns Into Something Else

If the test shows a failed line, the next step is emergency burst pipe repair. A tank that is weeping at the base is a water heater repair question. Water that has already soaked flooring or drywall is handled under water damage restoration, which runs on its own line. A leak between the meter and the house falls under water line repair, and a leaking pool is covered by pool leak detection.
