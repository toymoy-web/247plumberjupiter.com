Sewage Is Backing Up Into the House

Several drains backing up together, a toilet that gurgles when the shower runs, or sewage in a floor drain all point to the same place: the main drain line. If a clearing visit finds a break, root mass, or sag, the job moves from cleaning to repair.

Stop running water first. Every flush and every load of laundry adds to the backup.

Overview

What Sewer Line Repair Covers

Sewer line repair fixes a damaged section of the private line between the house and the public sewer. The work starts with a camera to find and measure the problem. Then the plumber chooses a method: dig and replace a short section, or install a liner inside the existing pipe.

Repair follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and repair is the right call when the damage is local. If the pipe is failing along most of its length, sewer line replacement is a different decision.

Jupiter Conditions

Who Owns What in Jupiter

The Loxahatchee River District is the wastewater provider for Jupiter, Tequesta, parts of Juno Beach, and parts of Jupiter Farms. Its rules state that the owner of a property is responsible for maintaining the private sewer on that property, which includes the lateral that connects the house to the District's main.

A backup that affects only your house points to your lateral. If neighbors on your street are also backing up, the blockage may be in the main, and that is the District's to clear.

In Hobe Sound the wastewater utility is different. South Martin Regional Utility serves the community, and some properties are on septic instead.

Methods

Spot Dig or Liner

Spot repair by digging

  • Replaces only the damaged section
  • Good for a short break or offset
  • Needs access and yard restoration
  • Used when the pipe has settled or sagged

Pipe lining

  • Installs a new pipe inside the old one
  • Little or no digging
  • Good for cracks and root entry
  • Needs a pipe that is not collapsed
Prep

What to Do While You Wait

Stop water use

No flushing, laundry, or dishwasher until the line is cleared.

Keep children away

Backed-up wastewater carries bacteria, so pets and kids should stay out of those rooms.

Find the cleanout

Know where the outdoor cleanout is, since that is the access point.

Photograph first

Take dated photos for your insurer before any cleanup.
