Overview

Burst Pipe Repair in Jupiter

Burst pipe repair replaces a section of a supply line that has split, cracked, or let go at a fitting. The plumber stops the flow, cuts out the failed section, installs new pipe and fittings that suit the existing material, and pressure-tests the repair before the visit ends.

In Jupiter a burst line is rarely about ice. It is far more often about age, a failed fitting, a storm, or a joint that was never right.

Jupiter Conditions

What Breaks Pipes Around Jupiter

Hard freezes are rare in Palm Beach County, so the usual suspects are age, vibration, corrosion, and storms. Wind and fallen trees can shift soil and strain buried lines. Hurricane Frances, for example, produced an estimated peak gust of 91 mph at Jupiter Inlet in September 2004 according to the National Weather Service.

When service is restored after a storm or a utility repair, pressure can surge through lines weakened by age. The Town of Jupiter Utilities Department publishes boil water notice information when a notice applies, so check the Town's site if water quality is in question after an event.

Homes built in the 1970s through 1990s, when Jupiter grew from 3,136 to 24,986 residents by Census count, often have supply lines now at the end of their service life.

Decision

Repair the Section or Replace the Run

A section repair fits when

  • One isolated failure caused it
  • The rest of the pipe is in good shape
  • The material is easy to match
  • Water can be shut off for a short time

Replacing the run fits when

  • Several leaks have appeared
  • The pipe is brittle or pitted
  • Fittings keep failing
  • You plan a bigger update soon

Process

How the Repair Goes

1

Stop the flow

The failed section is isolated and the water is shut off.

2

Locate the break

The split, crack, or failed joint is found and its cause noted.

3

Cut and replace

The bad section is cut out and new pipe and fittings are installed.

4

Pressure-test

The repaired line is pressurized to confirm it holds.

5

Advise

You hear whether the rest of the run is sound, with a price before any extra work.
