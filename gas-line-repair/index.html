Overview

What Gas Line Work Covers

Gas line work covers installing new gas piping for appliances, extending an existing line, repairing a damaged or leaking section, and pressure-testing a system. Common projects include a gas range, a dryer, a tankless or tank water heater, a pool heater, an outdoor kitchen, a fireplace, and a standby generator.

The work follows the Florida Building Code, Fuel Gas, which is based on the International Fuel Gas Code (IFGC), and plumbing connections follow the International Plumbing Code (IPC). Gas piping needs a permit and an inspection, and the pipe is pressure-tested before it is put in service.

Jupiter Conditions

Natural Gas, Propane, and Permits

Natural gas service in Palm Beach County comes from investor-owned gas utilities regulated by the Florida Public Service Commission, and availability varies by street. Many homes have no natural gas main at all and use propane from a tank instead, so the first question is which fuel the project will use.

Pipe sizing matters most for generators. A whole-house standby generator draws a lot of gas, and an undersized line or meter will limit it. Sizing is based on the appliance ratings and the length of the run.

Before any digging for an underground line, Florida's one-call center, Sunshine 811, locates buried utilities. Permits come from the Town Building Department in Jupiter, Palm Beach County in unincorporated areas, and Martin County in Hobe Sound.
