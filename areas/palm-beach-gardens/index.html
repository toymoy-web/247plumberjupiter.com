Neighborhoods

A Big City With More Than One Plumbing Story

John D. MacArthur planned Palm Beach Gardens in 1959 as a garden city on about 4,000 acres. It filled in around him. The city had 1,800 residents within about a year and roughly 7,000 by 1970, and it now covers around 55 square miles, so plumbing in one corner can look nothing like plumbing in another.

Homes built close to the original core in the 1960s and 1970s often carry original drain lines and older supply pipe. Communities farther west, around PGA National and the PGA Boulevard retail corridor that includes The Gardens Mall, are newer, with plumbing that fails in different ways: fittings, water heaters, and irrigation tie-ins.

Emergency

Emergency Plumber Palm Beach Gardens FL 33410 and 33418: Getting Through the Gate

A large share of Palm Beach Gardens sits behind gates, so an emergency call here starts with access. Give dispatch the community name, the guardhouse number if you have it, and any gate code, so the crew is not stopped at the entrance while water runs.

Then describe what is happening. Dampness at a slab edge in 33410, a burst line in 33418, or a drain backing up anywhere in the city gets the same 24/7 response.

Condo and townhome callers should also reach building management, since many associations keep their own after-hours contact and shutoff rules. A short call to both saves time once the crew is on site.

Utilities

Seacoast Utility Authority and Your Side of the Meter

Seacoast Utility Authority, started in 1955 by MacArthur, serves Palm Beach Gardens along with Lake Park, North Palm Beach, and Juno Beach for both water and sewer. That is one agency for both services across much of the city.

If the whole street loses pressure or a sewer main backs up in the road, call the utility. If the problem is inside your property line, such as a leak after the meter or a clog in your lateral, the 24/7 plumbing line handles it. Repairs follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and water heater changes inside city limits can need a permit from the city building division.

Storm Rain

Heavy Rain, Waterways, and Backups

MacArthur's plan called for hundreds of waterways, and the city's drainage is part of its identity. In September 2004, the National Weather Service reported locally severe rainfall flooding in Palm Beach Gardens during Hurricane Jeanne, three weeks after Frances.

The plumbing lesson is about the lowest outlets. During long rain, floor drains, cleanouts, and shower pans are where a stressed sewer line announces itself. If water rises in one of them, stop running water and call.

ZIP Codes

33410 and 33418: Two Parts of One City

The Gardens Mall sits in 33410, which includes the eastern and central parts of the city. PGA National sits in 33418, which includes much of the western side. Calls from both ZIP codes go to one line.

Older 33410 homes fit slab leak pinpointing and repair and cable and jetting service. Newer 33418 communities fit water heater repair and hidden leak location. A burst line anywhere falls under burst pipe repair.

The gated golf communities inside the city include PGA National, Mirasol, BallenIsles, Frenchman's Creek, Old Palm, and Evergrene. Each has its own gate and association, so say the community name when you call. Next door, 33408, 33403, 33458, 33477, and 33478 are covered for burst pipes and slab leaks by the same dispatch line.

Landmarks

The Gardens Mall, Downtown at the Gardens, and Eissey Campus

Retail along PGA Boulevard includes The Gardens Mall and Downtown at the Gardens, and Palm Beach State College's Eissey Campus is in the city. PGA National covers 2,340 acres and about 6,900 homes.

Frenchman's Forest Natural Area and the Karen T. Marcus Sandhill Crane Access Park are the green spaces most callers know. Naming one of these along with your community helps dispatch place you.
