The Area

What The Bluffs Is

Most of The Bluffs sits close to Juno Beach, with the Atlantic on the east, the Intracoastal on the west and US 1 running through it. One listing count puts it above 3,300 residences, built around 1986 at the median.

It is made up of smaller named sections, including Marina, Ocean North, Ocean South, River, River North, Lakes and Ridge. Nearby are the Jupiter Ridge Natural Area, a 271 acre preserve, and the Bluffs Marina.

Condo Living

When the Problem Is in a Shared Building

In a condo or townhouse, a leak can start in your unit and damage the one below, or start above and drip down on you. Whose responsibility the repair is depends on the association documents, which usually say where the owner's side stops.

Before calling, read your declaration or ask the manager. Often the pipes inside your walls are the owner's job, while shared risers and drain stacks belong to the association. Tell the plumber which one you think it is.

Shutoffs

Finding the Right Water Shutoff

Many buildings have a shutoff inside each unit, and some also have a valve controlled by building staff. Learn where yours is now. In an emergency, close your unit valve, then call management so the building can close a riser if needed.

Tell dispatch your building name, unit number and street. If water is reaching a neighbor, say so up front, because that changes what comes first.
