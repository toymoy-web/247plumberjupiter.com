Overview

Where the 24/7 Line Dispatches

The 24/7 line covers the Town of Jupiter and the communities around it, from the Intracoastal Waterway to the farm lots west of I-95. That spans two counties, Palm Beach and Martin, and several utilities, which is why local pages matter.

Jupiter itself has four ZIP codes: 33458 for central Jupiter, 33477 along the coast, 33469 on the Intracoastal side, and 33478 for Jupiter Farms and the west. The pages below cover the neighbors.

Tequesta

Tequesta, 33469

Tequesta is a village platted in the late 1950s with its own water utility. Older homes and riverfront condos share the village. Read the Tequesta page for how the village handles water and sewer.

Juno Beach

Juno Beach, 33408

Juno Beach is a barrier-island town of 1950s lots and oceanfront condos, where salt air shortens the life of exposed fittings. The Juno Beach page explains which utility serves which address.

North Palm Beach

North Palm Beach, 33408 and 33410

North Palm Beach is a planned village of canals and 1950s homes about ten miles south of Jupiter, with harbor condos along the water. The North Palm Beach page covers the housing mix and which utility serves the village.

Palm Beach Gardens

Palm Beach Gardens, 33410 and 33418

Palm Beach Gardens is a large planned city with older core neighborhoods and newer gated communities. See the Palm Beach Gardens page for ZIP-by-ZIP notes and gated-community access.

Jupiter Farms

Jupiter Farms, 33478

Jupiter Farms is unincorporated acreage where most homes run on private wells and septic tanks. The Jupiter Farms page explains what a pump or tank emergency looks like.

Hobe Sound

Hobe Sound, 33455

Hobe Sound is across the county line in Martin County, which changes the permit office and the utility. The Hobe Sound page lists those differences.

Across the Line

Working Across the County Line

Palm Beach County and Martin County each run their own permit office, health department records, and utility. A water heater swap in Jupiter goes through the Town building department. In Hobe Sound it goes through Martin County. The technician confirms the right office for your address.

That difference is also why each page lists the nearest utility. The first question in an emergency is often who owns the line that failed, and the answer changes at the county line.

First Steps

What to Do Before You Call, Wherever You Are

The first steps are the same in every town. Close the main shutoff, switch off the water heater if a hot-water line is involved, move valuables off wet floors, and photograph the damage. Then call with your street and ZIP code ready. If you smell gas, leave first and call 911.

Plumbing work in all seven areas follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). The local permit office differs by county.
