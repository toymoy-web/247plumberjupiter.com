County Line

Hobe Sound Is Martin County, and That Changes the Paperwork

Hobe Sound is an unincorporated community in Martin County, just across the line from Tequesta. Everything that depends on the county changes on the other side of it: the building department, the septic records, and the utility.

Permits come from the Martin County Building Department at 900 SE Ruhnke Street in Stuart, and water heater installation and replacement need one. South Martin Regional Utility is the sole municipal supplier of water, wastewater, and reclaimed water for Hobe Sound and the Town of Jupiter Island. Septic and well records are kept by the Florida Department of Health in Martin County.

Emergency Plumber Hobe Sound FL 33455: Martin County Calls at Any Hour

Hobe Sound is in Martin County, so a late-night call here comes with a few extra questions. Dispatch asks whether the address is in Hobe Sound or on Jupiter Island, because South Martin Regional Utility serves both and the permit office is Martin County's, not Jupiter's.

Housing

Old Hobe Sound and Newer Subdivisions

Hobe Sound grew out of a 1920s land boom, when the Olympia Improvement Corporation announced elaborate plans and the community was briefly renamed Picture City. The old center around US 1, Dixie Highway, and Bridge Road is still a collection of modest, mature houses.

Older houses there tend to have original supply and drain lines and a patchwork of past repairs. Newer subdivisions away from the old center have newer lines that fail in different ways, more often at fittings and fixtures.

Warning Signs

Signs Pipes in an Older House Are Aging

Rust-brown water

Hot water that runs orange for a minute after sitting often means corroding pipe.

Falling pressure

A gradual drop at several fixtures suggests supply pipe narrowing inside.

Repeated drain clogs

Rough pipe walls catch debris and roots keep coming back.

Green stains at fittings

Copper corrodes at joints and leaves a telltale green trace.

Past patch repairs

Mixed materials on one run usually mean earlier failures.

Storm History

Two Landfalls Next Door

Hurricane Frances came ashore on Hutchinson Island on September 5, 2004. Jeanne followed on September 26 and landed within about two miles of the same spot, according to hurricane records. That stretch of coast sits a short drive north of Hobe Sound.

Storms like that shift soil, snap roots, and shake supply lines loose. After any big storm, check for new leaks once pressure returns, because a joint that survived the wind can fail when the water comes back on.

Landmarks

Jonathan Dickinson State Park, the Wildlife Refuge, and Bridge Road

Jonathan Dickinson State Park covers about 11,500 acres in Martin County, and the Hobe Sound National Wildlife Refuge covers 1,035 acres. The Hobe Sound Nature Center is at 13640 SE Federal Highway, and Peck Lake Park is at 8108 SE Gomez Avenue.

Blowing Rocks Preserve and the public beach sit in the Town of Jupiter Island, which shares the Hobe Sound ZIP 33455. Bridge Road is the main route to the island.
