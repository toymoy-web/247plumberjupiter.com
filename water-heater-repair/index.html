Overview

Water Heater Repair for Jupiter Homes

Water heater repair means finding out why a tank has stopped heating or started leaking, then fixing the part that failed. On an electric tank that is often a heating element or thermostat. On a gas unit it can be the burner, the thermocouple, or the gas valve.

The first question is always whether the tank itself is sound. A failed part is a repair. A tank that has corroded through is a replacement, and the diagnosis tells you which one you have.

Jupiter Conditions

What Fails Water Heaters Around Jupiter

Many Jupiter water heaters sit in garages or utility closets, where heat and humidity speed up corrosion on the tank jacket and fittings. Salt air near the coast adds to that wear.

Storm-season power cuts add another variable. When power returns after an outage, a stressed heating element or thermostat can fail, which is why hot-water calls tend to spike after storms.

Replacement is regulated. The Town of Jupiter Building Department requires a permit for water heater replacement, and a change from a tank to a tankless unit requires plan review. A like-for-like swap usually closes out in one inspection.

The International Plumbing Code (IPC), which the Florida Building Code, Plumbing is based on, calls for a temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge pipe on storage water heaters. If that valve drips or the pipe is missing, say so on the phone.

Decision

Repair or Replace

Repair makes sense when

  • The tank is not leaking
  • The unit is under about 8 to 10 years old
  • A single part has failed
  • The repair costs well under a replacement

Replace makes sense when

  • Water is escaping from the tank body
  • The tank is past its typical 8 to 12 year life
  • Rust or sediment is heavy
  • Repairs are repeating

Prep

If the Tank Is Leaking

Cut the power

Switch off the breaker for an electric tank or turn the gas control to off.

Close the cold supply

The shutoff valve above the tank stops new water from filling it.

Check the drain line

Water from the relief valve discharge pipe means pressure or temperature, not necessarily a tank failure.

Protect the floor

Put towels or a pan down and photograph the leak for any claim.

Related Work

When the Problem Is Bigger Than the Tank

A tank that has failed completely may flood a closet. If water has reached flooring, water extraction and drying handles the drying. Wet walls near the heater may point to a supply line, which leak location work can trace. A burst connection is a job for emergency pipe repair. A tank that is beyond repair moves to water heater replacement, and on-demand units are covered under tankless water heater service.

In Hobe Sound and other Martin County areas, the permit office is different, so the technician confirms it when you call.
