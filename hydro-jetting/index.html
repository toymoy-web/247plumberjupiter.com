They Snaked It and It Clogged Again

A common story is a drain that clears with a cable and backs up again a few weeks later. Often the cable punched a hole through the blockage but left grease and scale clinging to the pipe wall. Each month the opening narrows until the line stops again.

That is the job hydro jetting is built for. It does not poke a hole. It scours the whole inside of the pipe.

Overview

What Hydro Jetting Is

Hydro jetting uses a hose with a rotating nozzle that sprays water at very high pressure in all directions as it moves down the line. The spray cuts grease, flushes sediment, and shreds soft roots, and the water carries the debris out through a cleanout.

A jetting visit usually starts and ends with a camera. The first pass checks that the pipe is sound enough, and the last pass confirms the line is clean. Cleanout access in building drains is called for by the International Plumbing Code (IPC), which is the model behind the Florida Building Code, Plumbing.

Comparison

Hydro Jetting or a Cable

Cable machine

  • Good for one clogged fixture
  • Breaks through a single blockage
  • Lower cost for simple jobs
  • Can leave grease film behind

Hydro jetting

  • Cleans the full width of the pipe
  • Removes grease, scale, and sediment
  • Suits repeat clogs and main lines
  • Needs a sound pipe, so a camera comes first

Jupiter Conditions

Why Jupiter Lines Clog Repeatedly

Fine roots from ficus hedges and shade trees work into small joint gaps and catch paper and grease. Restaurant and commercial kitchens add fats, oils, and grease that harden in the line. Older cast iron stacks build rough scale that traps debris.

The Loxahatchee River District treats wastewater for Jupiter, Tequesta, parts of Juno Beach, and parts of Jupiter Farms. Its rules make the property owner responsible for maintaining the private sewer on their property, so a lateral that keeps clogging is the owner's to fix. Jetting is one of the ways to keep it open.

Homes on septic tanks need a different plan. Jetting a line that leads to a full tank only moves the problem.

Process

What a Jetting Visit Includes

1

Camera first

A camera checks the pipe for cracks, sags, and roots before any pressure is used.

2

Access

A cleanout is opened, or a fixture is removed if no cleanout exists.

3

Jet the line

The nozzle is run through the line, working back and forth until the water runs clear.

4

Camera again

A second pass confirms the pipe wall is clean and shows any damage.

5

Advise

You get a maintenance schedule or a repair quote, with a price before more work.
