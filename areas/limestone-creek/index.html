The Area

Where Limestone Creek Is

The river runs just north of this ZIP 33458 subdivision. Listing sources describe detached houses with big backyards, and one agent says almost every home backs onto a lake or nature preserve.

Limestone Creek Park, a Palm Beach County park, and the Limestone Creek Natural Area sit beside it. The Natural Area has a hiking trail, an observation deck and a fishing pier, which gives the neighborhood a green edge.

Mixed Ages

Homes of Different Ages on the Same Streets

Limestone Creek homes were not all built at once, so two houses on the same street can have very different plumbing. We do not assume what is inside a given house. The first questions are the year it was built and what has been replaced.

If you are not sure, a technician can look at the visible supply lines, the shutoff valves and the water heater to see what you have. That is better than guessing from the neighborhood name.

Outdoors

Yards, Hose Bibs and Irrigation

Large backyards bring outdoor plumbing: hose bibs, irrigation lines and sometimes a pool. A wet patch of lawn, an unusually green stripe or a drop in sprinkler pressure can point to a leak in an outdoor line.

Because many lots here border lakes or preserve land, a soggy area is worth a look rather than a guess. A meter test with every tap closed tells you if water is leaving the house system at all.
