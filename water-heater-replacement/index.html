Overview

Replacing a Water Heater in Jupiter

Water heater replacement means removing a failed or aging unit and installing a new one that fits the household, the fuel supply, and the building code. The work covers the tank or tankless unit itself, the supply and drain connections, venting on gas models, the temperature and pressure relief valve, and the permit.

Most replacements start with a leak or a cold shower. A tank that is weeping from its body cannot be repaired, so the question shifts from what part failed to which unit should go in next.

Permits

What the Town Requires

In Jupiter, water heater replacement needs a permit from the Town Building Department at 210 Military Trail. The Town's form asks for the tank size, fuel, electrical load, and breaker size, and it states that a change from a tank to a tankless unit requires plan review.

The work follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). That code calls for a temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge pipe, and a drain pan with its own drain where a leak would damage the building. Both are routine on a proper swap and easy to spot when they are missing.

Outside town limits the permit office changes. Palm Beach County covers unincorporated areas such as Jupiter Farms, and Martin County covers Hobe Sound.

Jupiter Conditions

Why Tanks Fail Early Around Here

Garages and utility closets in Florida run hot and humid, and salt air near the coast adds corrosion to fittings and the tank jacket. A tank that sits in an attic can leak for weeks before a ceiling stain appears.

Storm-season outages play a part too. When power cycles back on, a tired element or thermostat can fail, which is one reason replacement calls rise after a storm.

Process

What a Replacement Visit Looks Like

1

Confirm

The old unit is checked to confirm it is beyond repair and the new size is agreed.

2

Drain and remove

The tank is shut off, drained, and disconnected, with water protected from floors.

3

Set and connect

The new unit is placed on a pan, with supply lines, relief valve, and venting connected.

4

Test

Pressure, temperature, venting, and leaks are checked before the visit closes.

5

Inspect

The permit inspection is scheduled, and a like-for-like swap often passes in one visit.

Around Jupiter

Replacing a Tank in Jupiter, Tequesta, and Jupiter Inlet Colony

Permits depend on the address. The Town of Jupiter Building Department at 210 Military Trail handles work inside town limits, and Tequesta and Jupiter Inlet Colony are separate municipalities with their own offices.

Jupiter Inlet Colony is a town of about 405 residents next to Tequesta, so a Colony address will not follow the Jupiter permit path. Give the street when you call and the permit office is confirmed before the tank is ordered.
