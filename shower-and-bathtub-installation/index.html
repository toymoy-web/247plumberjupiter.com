Overview

What Shower and Bathtub Installation Covers

Shower and bathtub installation covers replacing a tub, converting a tub to a shower, adding a shower, or replacing the shower valve and drain. The plumbing includes the valve and rough-in, the drain and trap, and the connections to the new fixture. Tile, glass, and finish carpentry are separate trades.

The plumber's role is to get the water and drain right behind the finish. That is where mistakes cost the most, because a leak behind new tile is expensive to find.

Jupiter Conditions

Slabs, Drains, and Who Is Doing the Remodel

Most South Florida houses sit on a concrete slab, so moving a shower drain means cutting the slab, laying new drain pipe, and pouring it back. That is the main cost driver in a tub-to-shower conversion. It is also why swapping a tub for a shower in the same spot is far cheaper than moving it.

Jupiter and Tequesta have an older population. ZIP 33469 has a median age of about 56, according to ZIP-Codes.com, and walk-in showers with low or curbless thresholds are common requests.

Plumbing work needs a permit, and Florida law requires licensed contractors for it. In a condo or HOA community, the association may also need to approve the work and set work hours, and some buildings restrict slab cutting.

Decision

Replace the Tub or Convert to a Shower

Replace the tub

  • Keeps the existing drain and layout
  • Lower plumbing cost
  • Suits families with children
  • Keeps a bath for resale appeal

Convert to a shower

  • Easier entry, fewer trips hazards
  • Frees up space in the bathroom
  • May need slab work for a new drain
  • Needs careful waterproofing
