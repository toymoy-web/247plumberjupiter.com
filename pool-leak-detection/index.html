Is My Pool Leaking or Just Evaporating?

Pools in Florida lose water every day to sun and wind. A drop of about a quarter inch a day is often normal evaporation in hot weather, and more than that deserves a look. A pool that needs the autofill running all the time, a water bill that has climbed, or wet ground near the equipment pad are common clues.

The bucket test is the simplest way to tell the difference. It uses evaporation as the control.

Test

The Bucket Test at Home

1

Fill a bucket

Fill a bucket with pool water to about an inch below the rim.

2

Set it on a step

Place it on a pool step so the bucket and the pool share the same water level and conditions.

3

Mark both levels

Mark the water level inside the bucket and on the outside of the bucket at the pool level.

4

Wait a day

Leave the pump running as normal and do not use the pool or top it off.

5

Compare

If the pool dropped more than the bucket, the pool is losing water to a leak.

Overview

What Pool Leak Detection Covers

Pool leak detection finds where water is escaping. Possible places include the skimmer and its connection, the return lines, the suction line to the pump, the equipment pad fittings, the light niche, the main drain, and the shell itself.

Methods include isolating each line with plugs, pressure-testing the lines, using listening equipment, and using dye in suspected spots. Pool plumbing leaks that sit in the pipe are the part a plumber fixes. Shell and finish issues are handled by pool contractors. Residential pool work in Florida falls under the Florida Building Code, and plumbing connections follow the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Pools, Heat, and Local Rules

Pools are common in the single-family neighborhoods around Jupiter, Palm Beach Gardens, and Jupiter Farms. Hot summers and frequent storms mean constant adjustments to the water level, which can hide a slow leak for months.

Summer thunderstorms and tropical rain can refill a leaking pool and mask the loss for days. A leak check is most reliable in dry stretches.

Underground pool lines in Jupiter sit in the same yard as irrigation and utilities. Before any digging, Sunshine 811 marks buried lines. If the leak affects a pool equipment pad and the pad connects to the house plumbing, a plumber and a pool contractor may both be involved.

Spas and Hot Tubs

Spa and Hot Tub Leaks

A spa that loses water faster than evaporation explains, or a damp patch near the equipment pad, can come from plumbing as well as the shell. Pressure testing the lines separates the two.

If the water level drops only when the jets run, the problem is usually on the pressure side of the system.
