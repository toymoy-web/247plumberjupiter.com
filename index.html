Local Conditions

What Plumbing Emergencies Look Like in Jupiter

Jupiter sits on the Atlantic coast of northern Palm Beach County, where the Loxahatchee River meets the Jupiter Inlet. Salt air and summer humidity are hard on exposed metal fittings, and hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30. An emergency plumber in Jupiter spends a lot of time on water heaters, supply lines, and shutoff valves that gave out at the worst hour, which is why water heater repair is one of the busiest calls.

Callers usually place themselves by a landmark. The Jupiter Inlet Lighthouse at 500 Captain Armour's Way has been lit since 1860, Roger Dean Chevrolet Stadium anchors Abacoa, and Harbourside Place sits at US 1 and Indiantown Road. Jupiter Medical Center is on South Old Dixie Highway, and the FAU John D. MacArthur Campus is on Parkside Drive. Giving dispatch the street, the ZIP, and the nearest of these gets a crew to the right door faster.

Many Jupiter homes sit on concrete slabs, so a supply line that fails often does so under the floor, out of sight. The first clues tend to be a water bill that jumps, a warm spot on a tile floor, or the sound of running water when every fixture is off. Those are leak detection calls, not guesswork calls, and a confirmed slab leak moves on to slab leak repair.

The Town of Jupiter Utilities Department supplies water to Jupiter, Juno Beach, and parts of unincorporated Palm Beach and Martin Counties. The Loxahatchee River District handles wastewater. That split matters at 3 a.m. A backup that starts in the street main is a call to the district, while one that starts in your own drain line is a plumbing job.

West of I-95 in Jupiter Farms, many homes run on private wells and septic systems instead of town water and sewer. A failed septic line or a dead well pressure tank is a different emergency from a town-served house, and the Jupiter Farms page explains the first move for each.

Know Your Utility Split

Water service comes from the Town of Jupiter Utilities Department. Wastewater comes from the Loxahatchee River District. Private wells and septic tanks stand alone in places like Jupiter Farms.

Knowing which system feeds your house tells you who to call first when the problem could sit on either side of the property line.

Emergency Prep

Before the Plumber Arrives

Close the main

Turn the main shutoff to closed: clockwise on a round handle, a quarter turn on a lever. The Town of Jupiter publishes a guide to finding it.

Kill the heater

Switch off power or gas to the water heater so the element cannot run dry.

Document it

Photograph the damage and the source before anything is moved or thrown out.

Smell gas? Leave

Get outside and call 911 first. A gas smell is not a plumbing call-line job.
