What This Is

A Phone Line for Plumbing Emergencies in Jupiter

Jupiter 24/7 Plumbing is a website and phone line built for one purpose: getting a person with a plumbing problem to the right help quickly. There are no online forms and no accounts. You read, then you call.

The site explains common problems, what they usually mean, and what to do before help arrives. It covers Jupiter, Tequesta, Juno Beach, North Palm Beach, Palm Beach Gardens, Jupiter Farms, and Hobe Sound.

Disclosure

Who Is Behind the Site

This website is independently operated and may be compensated when you call. Calls are connected to local plumbing service providers, and the provider that takes your call performs and prices the work. The site itself does not install, repair, or inspect anything.

Because the work is done by providers, the site does not publish a license number, reviews, or customer counts of its own. Ask whichever company you speak with for its Florida license and check it yourself on the Department of Business and Professional Regulation license search at myfloridalicense.com.

Limits

What This Site Does Not Do

It does not take service requests online, quote prices by text or email, or handle gas emergencies. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 before anything else.

It also does not replace advice from your insurer, an attorney, or an engineer. Pages on insurance, permits, and codes are general information about how things usually work in Florida, and they can change.

Standards

The Codes Behind the Work

Plumbing work in Florida follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). Contractors are licensed under Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes, and local building departments issue permits and inspect certain work, such as water heater replacement.

Water damage cleanup follows the IICRC S500 standard, a published guide that sorts water by contamination level and by how much material is wet. Mold work above 10 square feet is a separately licensed trade under Chapter 468, Part XVI of the Florida Statutes.

A Jupiter Farms home on a well and septic tank has little in common with a Juno Beach condo, and Hobe Sound is in a different county than Jupiter.
