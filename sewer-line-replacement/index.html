Overview

When Repair Is Not Enough

Sewer line replacement means removing the old lateral, or replacing it in place, and installing a new continuous pipe from the house to the public connection. It is the right answer when a camera shows damage along most of the pipe, not just in one spot.

A line that has cracked in several places, sagged over a long run, or turned to scale and rot rarely stays fixed with patches. Replacement ends the cycle of repeat backups.

Decision

Replace or Repair: How to Tell

These are the signs that push a camera finding toward replacement.

FindingPoints towardWhy
One crack or one offset jointRepairDamage is local and the rest of the pipe is sound.
Cracks in several placesReplacementPatching each one adds up and misses the next failure.
Long belly or sagReplacementThe pipe cannot drain without regrading.
Collapsed sectionReplacementLining needs an open pipe.
Repeated backups after jettingReplacementThe pipe wall is too rough or too narrow.
Methods

Trenching or Trenchless Replacement

Open trench

  • Digs the full length of the line
  • Lets the plumber regrade for fall
  • Works when the pipe has collapsed
  • Needs yard and driveway restoration

Trenchless pipe bursting

  • Pulls a new pipe through the old path
  • Uses small entry and exit pits
  • Spares lawns and hardscape
  • Needs the old pipe to be pullable

Jupiter Conditions

Sewer, Septic, and Timing in Jupiter

Sewer replacement in Jupiter sits inside a larger story. The Loxahatchee River District has converted nearly 4,000 homes from septic tanks to central sewer since it was created in 1971, according to a 2016 recognition by the South Florida Water Management District. A 2010 inventory found thousands of septic systems still remaining in its service area.

That matters for two reasons. If sewer is available on your street, a failing septic tank may be a candidate for connection, and the District's rules set a deadline for connecting once sewer is available. Confirm timing with the District. If you are already on sewer, the private lateral is yours to maintain.

Pipes in 1960s homes in Tequesta and the older parts of Jupiter are often cast iron or clay. Those materials are the most likely to be replaced instead of patched.

Process

How a Replacement Job Runs

1

Inspect

A camera maps the line and confirms the length, depth, and material.

2

Permit

The permit is pulled, and utility locates are requested through 811.

3

Replace

The new pipe is installed by trench or trenchless methods.

4

Reconnect

The new line is tied to the house cleanout and the public connection.

5

Inspect again

The camera runs the new line, and the building inspector signs off.
