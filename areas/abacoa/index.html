The Area

Where Abacoa Sits in Jupiter

Abacoa is in the southwest part of Jupiter, around Abacoa Town Center, Roger Dean Stadium and the Florida Atlantic University Harriet L. Wilkes Honors College. The MacArthur Foundation proposed the plan to Palm Beach County in 1993, and construction began in 1997 on land that had been leased for strawberry farming.

The community is built from many named neighborhoods, among them Osceola Woods, Valencia, Tuscany, Martinique, Mallory Creek, Somerset, Cambridge, Windsor Park, San Palermo, Canterbury Place, Greenwich, Charleston Court, Antigua and New Haven. New Haven came first, and buyers drew lots to choose homes.

Newer Community

Plumbing in a Community Built Since 1997

Abacoa is far newer than most of Jupiter, with the first homes dating to the late 1990s. That does not tell us what is inside any one house. Some owners still have original fixtures and others have replaced them, so the useful question is what was installed and when.

Keep the paperwork for any heater, fixture or repair. If a part is still under a manufacturer warranty, checking before work begins can save a bill, and a clear repair history helps a technician narrow down a problem faster.

Attached Homes

Townhouses, Shared Walls and Rentals

Much of Abacoa is townhouses and attached homes, plus rental apartments. In an attached home a leak can reach the neighbor, so the first moves are to close the unit's main valve and tell the association or property manager.

Renters usually should reach the landlord or manager first, because the owner often decides who does repairs. Owners and managers can call to ask about service for a unit, a building or several rentals.

Common Calls and First Moves

General guidance for any home. A technician confirms the cause.
