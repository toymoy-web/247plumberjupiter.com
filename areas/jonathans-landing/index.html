The Area

Where Jonathan's Landing Sits

Alternate A1A forms one edge and the Intracoastal Waterway the other, just south of Indiantown Road in ZIP 33477. The grounds cover roughly 600 acres, part of it lakes and waterways, and there are three gated entrances.

The community is divided into 27 villages. Real estate sources count 18 single-family villages, five condominium villages and four townhome villages, with homes built between 1979 and 2007 and a golf club on site.

Gated Access

Getting a Plumber Through the Gate

In a gated community a plumber cannot just drive in. Tell the gate that a service visit is expected, or put the visitor on your list ahead of time, and give the plumber the village name and street.

Some villages are condominium or townhome groups with their own managers, so ask whether the association needs notice for work. Having the answer ready keeps a visit from stalling at the entrance.

Villages

Different Villages, Different Build Years

Because homes here were built over almost three decades, villages differ in age. Sources do not tell us which village has which plumbing, so we do not assume anything. Knowing your village and when it was built gives a technician a place to start.

Waterfront and golf-course lots also mean outdoor plumbing, such as irrigation, hose bibs and sometimes a dock water line. Those lines have their own shutoffs, worth finding before a leak.
