Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026. This policy explains what information this website and phone line collect and how it is used.

    Summary

    The Short Version

    This website has no forms, accounts, or comment fields. The main information we receive comes from phone calls. We do not sell your personal information.

    Calls

    Information Collected When You Call

    When you call the number on this site, the call may be routed through a call-tracking service. That service can record the time and length of the call, the number you called from, the number you dialed, and the page that led you to call.

    Calls may be recorded or monitored for quality, training, and record keeping. You can ask the person who answers whether a call is being recorded. If you do not want a call recorded, do not continue the call.

    What you say on a call, such as your name, address, and description of the problem, is used to connect you with a provider and to handle your request.

    Website

    Information Collected When You Browse

    This site does not use advertising cookies and does not run analytics scripts. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which can include your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested.

    Fonts are hosted on this site. Every page embeds a Google Map near the footer, and when it loads, Google can receive your IP address and may set cookies under its own privacy policy.

    Use

    How Information Is Used

    Information is used to answer calls, connect you with a plumbing provider, measure which pages lead to calls, keep the site working, prevent fraud and abuse, and meet legal obligations.

    Sharing

    Who Receives Information

    Call details may be shared with the plumbing providers who take your call, with call-tracking and telephone service vendors, and with the hosting provider. We may also share information when the law requires it or to protect rights and safety.

    Providers who take your call handle your information under their own policies.

    Keeping

    Retention and Security

    Call records are kept as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal and business requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

    Choices

    Your Choices

    You can choose not to call, ask whether a call is recorded, or ask to see or delete call information associated with your number by emailing contact@247plumberjupiter.com. You can block cookies in your browser settings, which may affect embedded maps.

    This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

    Updates

    Changes and Contact

    We may update this policy and will change the effective date above when we do. Questions can be sent to contact@247plumberjupiter.com.

    Questions About Privacy?

    Email contact@247plumberjupiter.com or call the 24/7 line.
