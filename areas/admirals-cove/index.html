The Area

What Admirals Cove Is

Building began in the mid-1980s, with canals that connect to the Intracoastal Waterway. A listing source counts about 895 homes, with some 300 on the water and 160-plus beside fairways.

The community has two 18-hole courses and one nine-hole course, and its own marina. Most homes are single-family, with condominiums and townhouses also in the mix, and the Town of Jupiter's neighborhood map lists both Admirals Cove and Admirals Cove Golf Village.

Larger Homes

What a Bigger House Changes

A house of several thousand square feet has more bathrooms, more fixtures and longer pipe runs than a typical home. That does not mean more problems. It means a leak can be harder to place and can cause more damage before it shows.

Know where the main shutoff is, and look for separate shutoffs for wings, guest suites or outdoor kitchens. When you call, say which part of the house shows the problem, because that narrows the search.

Waterfront and Golf Lots

Outdoor Plumbing and Access

Waterfront and golf lots usually come with irrigation, outdoor kitchens or pool equipment, each with its own lines. A dock may also have a water supply. Tell dispatch about any outdoor system involved.

As in any gated community, arrange entry with the gate ahead of time. Give the plumber your street, and say if the home has a villa association that needs notice for work.
