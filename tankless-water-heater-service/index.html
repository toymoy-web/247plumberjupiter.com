My Tankless Heater Is Not Giving Hot Water

Tankless owners rarely say service. They say the water goes lukewarm, the unit shows a code, or it clicks and shuts off. Many of those problems come down to scale inside the heat exchanger, a blocked inlet filter, or a vent or gas supply issue.

A tankless heater heats water only when you open a tap, so a failure is felt right away. That is why a correct diagnosis matters more than a quick reset.

Overview

How Tankless Service Differs From Tank Repair

A tankless water heater has no storage tank, so there is no tank to corrode. The parts that fail are the heat exchanger, flow sensor, igniter or elements, control board, and the vent and gas or electrical supply. Service starts with the error code and a flow and temperature check.

The flush is the main maintenance task. Circulating a descaling solution through the unit clears mineral buildup that narrows the heat exchanger and triggers codes.

Jupiter Conditions

Water, Venting, and Permits in Jupiter

Jupiter's Town water is treated by nanofiltration and reverse osmosis, according to the Town Utilities Department, so scale can build more slowly than in some Florida cities. Customers on private wells in Jupiter Farms are different, because well water can carry hardness and iron that foul a heat exchanger faster.

Gas models need a clear vent path, which matters in older garages and closets. Electric models need a circuit sized for the load, which can mean an electrical permit as well as a plumbing permit.

The Town of Jupiter requires a permit for water heater replacement, and a change from a tank to tankless needs plan review. Hobe Sound uses Martin County instead.

Decision

Repair, Flush, or Replace

A flush or repair fits when

  • The unit is under about 10 years old
  • A single part has failed
  • A code points to scale or a filter
  • The rest of the system is sound

Replacement fits when

  • The heat exchanger has failed
  • Repairs keep coming back
  • The unit is near the end of its typical 15 to 20 year life
  • Parts are no longer available
